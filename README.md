## PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project analyzes customer reviews from an e-commerce clothing retailer to extract meaningful insights about product performance and customer satisfaction. The dataset includes 23,486 customer reviews with attributes like star ratings, recommendations, positive feedback count, and product categories.

## GOAL
To analyze customer reviews and ratings to understand product performance, customer satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## OBJECTIVES
- Assess customer satisfaction through average product ratings and feedback.
- Measure engagement by tracking positive feedback count.
- Evaluate recommendation trends across product categories.
- Compare performance across divisions and classes.
- Identify patterns in feedback based on customer age categories.

## TOOLS
- Power BI – For creating dynamic dashboards and visualizations
- Microsoft Excel – For initial data inspection and formatting
- DAX – For calculated metrics and KPIs within Power BI
- Power Query – For data cleaning and shaping

## KPIs
The following KPIs were used to measure customer satisfaction and product performance:
1. Average Rating – The overall average rating of all reviewed products.
2. Total Positive Feedback – The sum of positive feedback received on all reviews.
3. Average Positive Feedback – The average number of positive feedback per review
4. Total Products Reviewed – The total count of unique products reviewed.
5. Percentage of Recommended Products – The percentage of reviews where the customer recommended the product.

## INSIGHTS
- Top 5 feedbacks by classname: Dresses received higher positive feedback than others.This helps in product production and inventory management
- Recommended products by division name:  General & General petite had a higher percentage of recommendation.
- Rating Distribution: Shows distribution with most ratings in the 4-5 star range
- Positive feedback by age category: Adult gave the most positive feedback while Youth gave the least positive feedback
- Star rating by class name: Dresses had consistent high ratings while Lounge had a consistent low rating.

## RECOMMENDATION
1. **Fix Low-Rated Products:**  
   Review and improve products that received 1–2 star ratings based on customer complaints.

2. **Encourage Older Customers to Leave Feedback:**  
   Offer small incentives (discounts or loyalty points) to encourage more reviews from older customers.

3. **Promote Best-Rated Products:**  
   Feature high-rated products in marketing materials and on the website to increase conversions.

4. **Pay Attention to Negative Reviews:**  
   Analyze poor reviews to find common issues and improve the customer experience.

5. **Track Changes Over Time:**  
   Regularly monitor ratings and feedback to see if changes lead to better results.
